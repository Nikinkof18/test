HOW FARM ECONOMICS WORK

Net impact = gross kitchen rewards + farm sales − kitchen rewards you give up by spending dishes on feeding.

Kitchen production and its displayed rewards remain gross. Farm feed consumption is used to calculate the opportunity cost, and the farm's net impact is included in the time-to-buy estimates in My Kitchen. Product sales are estimates based on your average feeds per day.

Animal counts are only simulation inputs: adding or removing animals never charges your Service Coin balance. This simplified model assumes each adult animal produces one item per feed cycle and that all items are sold at the configured price.

HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

How are rewards calculated?

Every 1,000 dishes produced gives 5 Service Coins and 5 Azure. 10,000 Azure equals $1.

How does Azure reinvestment work?

When enabled, Azure is treated as reinvested into Service Coins at a 10% bonus: 1 Azure becomes 1.1 Service Coins. This is used when calculating the time to reach the next chef.

How do ad rewards work?

Each ad gives 25 Service Coins instantly, up to 10 ads (250 Service Coins) per day. The calculator assumes the selected ads are watched immediately, and for longer estimates adds the same bonus again every 24 hours.

CryptoChef Calculator is an independent community calculator. Game values can be changed in data.js.
